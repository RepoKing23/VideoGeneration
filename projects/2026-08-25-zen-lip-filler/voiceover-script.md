## If you use a voiceover

Drop the on-screen narrative lines and keep only **Before** and **After** —
spoken words plus matching text on screen makes a reel feel busy, which fights
the calm you're going for. Say the word and I'll re-cut it that way.

Send the recording as a `.wav` or `.m4a` and I'll place it, duck the music
underneath automatically, and sync the captions to your actual delivery rather
than the estimated timings above.

## A note on wording

The lines avoid promising a specific medical outcome or a duration of effect,
which keeps you clear of the advertising rules most regions apply to cosmetic
procedures. If your clinic has approved claim wording, send it and I'll work
from that instead.
